This website does not use advertising cookies or build individual visitor profiles.

When privacy-friendly analytics are enabled, they are used only to understand aggregate traffic such as page views, referring websites, visitor countries, and site performance. Analytics are not displayed publicly and are not used to identify individual visitors.

External links, including GitHub and LinkedIn, are governed by the privacy policies of those services.
